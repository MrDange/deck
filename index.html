10 · Plan · Risks

As filed on Backable

Three risks, and how each one is handled

Risk

What would have to go wrong

How we handle it

Adoption against established wallets

Users see no reason to leave the incumbents

A wallet’s business model is known: swap fees, referrals, staking, rev share with projects. The open question is adoption, the one variable this raise tests. The first six months fund integrations and campaigns that show, in a minute, how much simpler the wallet feels, and record what sticks.

Platform policies and regulation

A store or regulatory change blocks a Powerup somewhere

The modular architecture lets the offering be adapted by service and by platform, so one restriction does not take the wallet down with it. Powerups can be withheld where they cannot run.

Market activity and revenue

A bear market cuts swap and service revenue

The monthly limit assumes no revenue from these services. Any revenue extends the runway rather than being needed to reach it, so a slow market changes the horizon, not the plan.
